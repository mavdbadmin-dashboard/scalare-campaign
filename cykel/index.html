Regulatory Disclosure

KEY RISK FACTORS

Summary only. Read the full Risk Factors section in the FCA-approved Prospectus before investing.

RISK 01
Revenue Stage
Limited trading history; no assurance that revenue targets will be achieved on projected timelines.
RISK 02
Going Concern
Auditor noted going concern for Jan 2025 year-end; subsequently addressed by £4.05M post-year fundraise.
RISK 03
Technology Dependency
Reliance on Crowdform Limited partnership and third-party LLM providers for core platform delivery.
RISK 04
Carrier/Shipper Adoption
Relay Freight AI requires API cooperation from carriers and operators; adoption timelines uncertain.
RISK 05
Competitive Risk
Microsoft, Salesforce, and established logistics technology providers represent well-resourced competition.
RISK 06
AI Regulatory Risk
Evolving regulatory frameworks across UK, EU, and US jurisdictions may affect product deployment and scope.
RISK 07
Revenue Concentration
Lucy currently the primary revenue driver; diversification across Eve and Samson is underway but not yet proven.
RISK 08
Key Person Risk
Small core team dependent on the continued involvement of Directors; loss of key personnel could be disruptive.
RISK 09
Dilution Risk
Issuance of new ordinary shares under this Placing will proportionately dilute the interests of existing shareholders.
RISK 10
Liquidity Risk
Shares are admitted to the LSE Main Market; trading volumes may be limited; the market may not be liquid.
