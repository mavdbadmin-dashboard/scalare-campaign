The Market Is $36 Billion — And Every Year, Banks Are Legally Compelled to Spend More

$36.8B Global KYC & AML market by 2035 · 22.3% CAGR

This is not a market banks can choose to exit. Anti-money laundering and know-your-customer compliance is legally mandatory across every major financial jurisdiction. When regulators fine a bank for compliance failures, they do not accept "we cut the budget" as a defence. The result is one of the most structurally defensible demand pools in enterprise software: a customer base that cannot reduce spend, in a market that is growing by double digits every year.

The regulatory tailwinds are accelerating. AUSTRAC in Australia levied a record A$1.3 billion fine against Westpac in 2020 for 23 million breaches of anti-money laundering law. The EU's Anti-Money Laundering Authority (AMLA) launched in 2024 with direct supervisory powers across the bloc. FinCEN in the United States continues to expand the scope of the Bank Secrecy Act. Every new regulation creates new mandatory spend — and every dollar of that spend flows to companies like FrankieOne.

FrankieOne aggregates more than 350 data sources — identity documents, biometric verification, sanctions lists, transaction monitoring, adverse media — into a single unified API. Financial institutions connect once. The platform handles the rest. For compliance officers managing global operations across dozens of jurisdictions, this is not a nice-to-have. It is the difference between regulatory survival and a nine-figure fine.
